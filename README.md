# Sales & Operations Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

An interactive **Microsoft Excel dashboard** built to analyze sales performance and operational efficiency across regions, product categories, products, order statuses, and delivery performance.

The project simulates an e-commerce operations environment and demonstrates how raw order data can be transformed into actionable business insights using Excel.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was designed to help answer key business questions such as:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- Which regions and categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which products are the strongest revenue drivers?
- What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
- Which regions have the best delivery performance?
- What is the cancellation rate?
- Which products have higher cancellation risk?
- How many orders are currently pending?
- How does revenue change over time?

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Regional Performance

South was the highest-revenue region, contributing **26.43% of total net sales**, with approximately **₹6.43L** in revenue.

### 2. Delivery Performance

West recorded the highest on-time delivery rate at **89.74%**, while North recorded the lowest at **88.18%**. The difference between the two regions was only **1.56 percentage points**, indicating relatively consistent delivery performance across regions.

### 3. Cancellation Risk

Sneakers generated approximately **₹1.99L in net sales** but had **₹40.2K in cancelled sales**, equivalent to **20.23% of their net sales**. This makes the product a potential area for further investigation.

### 4. Product Performance

Coffee Maker was the highest-revenue product, generating approximately **₹2.65L** and contributing **10.88% of total net sales**.

### 5. Fulfillment Backlog

**153 out of 1,000 orders (15.30%)** remained pending, indicating a significant volume of orders still in the fulfillment pipeline.
